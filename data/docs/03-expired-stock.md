# Expired and Near-Expiry Stock

What to do with lots that are close to their expiration date or already expired.

## Daily check

Every morning the shift lead reviews Inventory > Reporting > Expiring Lots, which lists
the lots in stock that are expired or expire within the warning window (30 days by
default). A summary email with the same list is sent at 7:00.

## Near-expiry lots (1 to 7 days left)

- Chilled lots with 1 to 7 days left are offered to the sales team for a discount sale
  or to the food bank partner. Sales must answer within 24 hours.
- Move the lot to the "Clearance" shelf of the cold room so pickers see it first.
- If the lot cannot be sold or donated before it expires, it will be scrapped.

## Expired lots

Expired products must never be delivered or donated.

1. Move the expired lot to WH/Quarantine with an internal transfer the same day.
2. Create a scrap order in Odoo (Inventory > Operations > Scrap) with the lot, the
   quantity and the reason "Expired".
3. Physically separate the goods in the red scrap cage and label them "DO NOT USE".
4. The quality manager signs the scrap form; the waste company collects it weekly.

The value of scrapped stock is reported monthly to finance. Repeated expiries of the
same product must be reviewed with purchasing to adjust order quantities.
